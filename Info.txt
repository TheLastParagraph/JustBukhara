Bakery is an important part of the business

This is worth emphasizing.

Just Bukhara appears to be more than a conventional restaurant.

The business-registration classification specifically identifies it with:

Biscuits + cakes + pastries + rusks

while customer reviews repeatedly mention:

cakes + sweets + confectionery + bakery products.

So a more accurate description would be:

Restaurant + Bakery + Confectionery + Sweets + Food/Takeaway outlet.
