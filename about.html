Our Journey

The Story of Just Bukhara

From our registration as a micro-enterprise to becoming one of Magam's most celebrated dining destinations.

Rooted in Magam.

Officially incorporated on August 4, 2022, as a proprietorship micro-enterprise, Just Bukhara was established with a dual focus: delivering exceptional restaurant services and manufacturing fresh bakery products on-site.

Since our doors opened, we have focused on providing generous portions, courteous service, and a cozy atmosphere that makes every family meal or quick takeaway a memorable experience. Today, we are proud to be a local staple located opposite the Indian Oil Petrol Pump on Gulmarg Road.

Culinary Experience

The Restaurant

Our restaurant side is celebrated for our signature Tandoori Chicken, rich Butter Chicken, and traditional Kashmiri Wazwan. We focus on providing high-quality ingredients, resulting in dishes that our community praises for both taste and value.

On-site Manufacturing

The Bakery

We are fully registered for the manufacture of biscuits, cakes, pastries, and rusks. Our bakery team crafts everything fresh daily, ensuring our famous basrakh and special occasion cakes always meet our strict standards for quality and freshness.
